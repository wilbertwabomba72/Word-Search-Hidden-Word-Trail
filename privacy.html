Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

This page explains what information Hidden Word Trail does and does not collect when you play the game.

Hidden Word Trail is designed to be a simple, relaxing word game. We do not require account creation, and we do not ask players to submit personal information in order to use the app.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location through the gameplay experience.

The app may store local gameplay data on your device, such as unlocked levels, star progress, and music settings, so that your progress is preserved between sessions.

Crash Reports and Technical Data

If Apple platform services provide crash reports or technical diagnostics, Apple may process technical information such as device type, operating system version, or crash logs. Any such data is governed by Apple’s own privacy practices.

How Data Is Used

  • To save game progress locally on your device
  • To maintain app stability and diagnose technical issues
  • To improve gameplay quality and future updates

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal information with third parties for advertising purposes through the game itself.

Children’s Privacy

Hidden Word Trail does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal data has been provided in error, please contact the developer through the support information listed on the app’s product page.

Data Retention

Gameplay settings and progress remain on your device unless you remove the app or clear device data associated with it.

Your Choices

You can stop using the app at any time. If you remove the app from your device, locally stored game progress may also be removed by the system.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product changes or legal requirements. Updated versions will be posted with a revised effective date.
